SupiriPOS PRO User Guide

1. 3 Landing Gateways

• Launch Instant Demo Shop: 1-click test of pre-populated Colombo Supermarket (PT-DEMO).
• Register Business: 4-step wizard to register your shop, upload your receipt logo, and get your unique Business Code (e.g. PT-00001).
• Terminal Unlock: Locks this terminal station to your business code.

2. Two-Stage Login Security

• Stage 1 (Terminal Unlock): Business Code + Business Master Password.
• Stage 2 (POS Staff Login): Default username user, password 123. Displays your shop logo and business badge.

3. Fast Cashier Billing & Printing

• Scan barcodes with any USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner; pressing Enter automatically adds the item to the active bill.
• Use quick cash tender buttons (+500, +1,000, +5,000, Exact) to instantly calculate Change Due (ඉතුරු මුදල).
• Complete sale triggers thermal receipt print (80mm / 58mm) and deducts inventory in real time across all counters.

4. Real-Time Multi-Device Sync

• Connected via WebSockets. When Cashier 1 makes a sale or adjusts stock, Counter 2 and Counter 3 automatically update without refreshing!
