SupiriPOS PRO User Guide & Documentation

Comprehensive Manual • Powered by PrasaTek System Solutions
Welcome to SupiriPOS PRO!

A next-generation multi-tenant cloud Point of Sale engineered for Sri Lankan supermarkets, grocery chains, bakeries, hardware shops, and retail stores with real-time Supabase cloud synchronization and Resend email security.

1. Landing Gateways & Terminal Login

• Instant Live Demo: Test the pre-populated Colombo Supermarket (PT-DEMO) with one click without registering.
• Register Business: 4-step wizard to register your shop, configure receipt details, upload your logo, and generate your permanent Business Code (e.g., PT-00001).
• Terminal Unlock (Stage 1): Locks the workstation browser to your unique Business Code using your Master Password.

2. Cashier Staff Login & Mandatory First-Time Credential Change

• Default Staff Account: New businesses start with initial credentials (Username: user, Password: 123).
• Mandatory Credential Change: Upon first login, SupiriPOS automatically requires setting a permanent username, secure password, and owner's full name. The temporary user/123 account is permanently disabled.
• Granular Staff Permissions: Store managers can create cashier or accountant accounts with restricted access to specific POS tabs (Billing, Inventory, Users, Reports).

3. Fast Cashier Billing, Barcode Scanning & Thermal Printing

• Barcode Scanner Integration: Connect any USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner; scanned items are automatically added to the active cart upon pressing Enter.
• Cash Tender Hotkeys: Click quick cash buttons (+500, +1,000, +5,000, Exact) to instantly calculate Change Due (ඉතුරු මුදල).
• Thermal Receipt Printing: Instant 80mm and 58mm thermal receipts with custom shop logos, itemized totals, discount notes, and Sinhala text support.
• Real-Time WebSocket Sync: All terminal stations and registers share live inventory counts and sales updates instantly without page reloading.

4. Business Profile Management & Resend Email OTP Security

• Updating Store Details: Store name, physical address, contact phone, receipt footer, and logo can be modified from either the ⚙️ Settings tab or by clicking your Username in the top header.
• Resend Email OTP Verification: To protect against unauthorized changes, clicking "Save Changes" generates an encrypted 6-digit OTP code sent directly to the registered business owner's email address via the Resend HTTPS API (noreply@prasatek.lk).
• Permanent Store Deletion (Danger Zone): Requires typed confirmation (DELETE <BUSINESS_CODE>) plus an explicit Resend deletion OTP code to prevent accidental data loss.

5. Support Ticket System (POS Dropdown & Public Landing Page)

• From Inside the POS: Click on your Username in the top header and select "🎧 Create Support Ticket". Pre-fills your store code and submits your technical query immediately.
• From the Public Landing Page: Anyone or locked-out merchants can click "🎧 Need Help? / Support" on the landing page or terminal unlock screen and submit a ticket using their Registered Email Address.
• Direct Email Confirmation & Replies: You receive an instant confirmation email via Resend. When PrasaTek technical engineers reply from the Master Admin Panel, the response is delivered straight to your email inbox!

6. Stealth Master Admin Backdoor & System Administration

• Hidden Super Admin Access: Entering admin@prasatek.lk (default password: PrasaTek@2026) into the standard Terminal Login form opens the Master Admin Control Panel.
• Dynamic Database Sync: Login credentials dynamically query PostgreSQL in Supabase on every attempt with no hardcoded fallbacks.
• Platform Governance: Master Admin can view all registered businesses, provision new stores, suspend/activate accounts, review support tickets, and email official replies.
